# Installing Servalan

Download the installer for your computer from [Releases](../../../releases). Servalan runs on Windows and macOS. It needs an internet connection for its maps and for heights on routes you draw.

## Why you'll see a warning

The feedback builds aren't signed yet. A signed app carries a certificate that tells Windows or macOS who made it. Without one, your computer can't check, so it warns you the first time you open the app. You only need to do the steps below once.

## Windows

1. Double-click `Servalan-<version>-windows.exe`.
2. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer's steps. Servalan is then in the Start menu.

## macOS

1. Open `Servalan-<version>-macos.dmg` and drag **Servalan** into **Applications**.
2. Open Applications. **Right-click** (or Control-click) Servalan and choose **Open**.
3. If macOS says Servalan **"can't be opened"** or **"cannot be checked for malicious software"**, choose **Open** if it is offered. If it isn't:
   1. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**.
   2. Scroll down to the message about Servalan and choose **Open Anyway**.
   3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

After the first time, Servalan opens normally.

## Updating

Download the new installer from Releases and install it over the old one. Your routes are kept, because they are stored separately from the app.

## Removing Servalan

- **Windows:** Settings, then Apps, then Servalan, then Uninstall.
- **macOS:** drag Servalan from Applications to the Bin.

Removing the app doesn't remove your routes. Before you remove it, you can back them up: Settings, then Your data, then Back up library.
