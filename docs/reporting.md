# Reporting a problem

[Open an issue](../../../issues/new/choose) and choose **Something's wrong** or **An idea**.

These details help us most:

- **Your Servalan version.** It's in **About Servalan**: in the Help menu on Windows, or the Servalan menu on macOS. It's also at the foot of Settings.
- **Your computer:** Windows or macOS, and which version.
- **What you did, step by step,** what you expected, and what happened instead.
- **A screenshot**, if it shows the problem.
- **The route**, if the problem is about a particular route and you're happy to share it. Use File, then Download as GPX Route, and attach the file. Remember that a GPX of a ride from your door shows where you live.

**Please never post your OS key.** If you posted one by mistake, delete it from your OS Data Hub project and make a new one.

Issues here are public, so anyone can read what you write.
