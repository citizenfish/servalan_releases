## Data

- **OpenStreetMap:** map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
- **Ordnance Survey:** OS maps are shown with your own OS Data Hub key, under Ordnance Survey's terms. Grid conversion uses OS's OSTN15 transformation.
- **Terrain heights:** heights for routes you draw come from the Terrain Tiles open dataset on AWS. The sources it combines are credited in the app while heights are shown. The full list is at [tilezen/joerd attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md).
